FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about timelines, stacks, or pricing? Find clear answers below.

What types of web development projects do you handle?

We build everything from custom high-converting marketing websites, portfolios, and e-commerce stores to complex SaaS web applications, client portals, and REST API backends.

What tech stack do you recommend for new web applications?

We specialize in modern JavaScript frameworks (React, Next.js, Node.js) paired with fast databases (MongoDB, SQLite, PostgreSQL). For custom dynamic sites, we also utilize PHP / Laravel and WordPress depending on your team's needs.

How long does a standard web project take from kick-off to launch?

High-converting business websites typically launch in 2 to 4 weeks. Custom web applications and complex e-commerce stores take 4 to 8 weeks depending on features and scope.

Do you provide post-launch maintenance and technical support?

Yes! We offer monthly maintenance packages covering core web vitals speed tuning, security audits, software updates, content changes, and 24/7 uptime monitoring.

Can you redesign our existing website without losing SEO rankings?

Absolutely. We conduct full SEO url mapping, preserve 301 redirects, improve schema markup, and boost core web vitals so your organic traffic increases post-launch.
